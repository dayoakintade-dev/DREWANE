This policy explains how DREWANE collects, uses, and protects your information.

Shipping Returns Privacy
01

Privacy at DREWANE

We collect information needed to provide our services, process orders, and improve your experience.

We aim to collect only what we need and handle it responsibly.

02

Information We Collect

Information we collect may include:

  • Name and contact information.
  • Shipping and billing information.
  • Account and order details.
  • Messages you send to us.
  • Website and device information.
03

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Process and deliver orders.
  • Manage accounts and preferences.
  • Respond to customer service requests.
  • Send important order and service updates.
  • Improve our website and services.
  • Help prevent fraud and misuse.
04

Cookies & Website Data

DREWANE may use cookies to remember preferences, improve functionality, and understand website usage.

05

Sharing Information

We may share information with trusted providers such as payment, delivery, technology, and customer service partners when needed to operate our business.

We do not sell your personal information as a source of revenue.

06

Payments & Security

Payment information is handled through appropriate payment-processing services. DREWANE does not intentionally store complete payment card details.

We use reasonable measures to help protect your information.

07

Your Choices & Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or request a copy of certain personal information.

You may also unsubscribe from optional marketing communications.

08

Data Retention

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain records, resolve disputes, and meet legal requirements.

Shipping & Delivery

Everything you need to know about receiving your DREWANE order.

01

Order Processing

Orders are prepared after they are placed. Please allow 1–2 business days for processing.

Weekend and holiday orders begin processing on the next business day.

02

Delivery Options

Service Estimated Delivery Cost
Standard 5–7 business days $15
Express 2–3 business days $35
03

Order Tracking

Once shipped, you'll receive a confirmation email with your tracking information.

Tracking may take some time to update.

04

International Shipping

Availability and costs vary by destination.

Duties, taxes, and customs charges are the customer's responsibility unless stated otherwise.

05

Delivery Questions

If your order has not arrived within the expected window, contact client services.
